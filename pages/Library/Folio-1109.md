**The Vim Bin** (lab item) +3 Safety, +1 Warping	
PeVi40 (PeVi30 + unlimited uses).
Dispel any magical effect on any item or substance which enters the bin 
up to level 22½ + stress die.

**The Blanket of Healing Sleep** +15 recover, provided the patient
is in bed, covered by the blanket at sunset and sunrise.
Side effect: psychedelic, but pleasant dreams.
Base 10 +1 touch +2 sun +1 lvl for 2 uses.

**Safe Cauldron**	+1 GQ, +1 Experimentation
CrIg30. The cauldron gently heats the contents,
and the temperature can easily be adjusted by concentrating.
Side effect: the cauldron gives off a smell of saffron, with intensity 
increasing with the heat.
